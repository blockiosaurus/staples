Write this week's update for the company and save it.

Follow the `staples-weekly-update` skill. In short:

1. Read the company with your read tools: `staples_get_briefing` (what needs the person, what is moving, what finished, and this month's spend against the budget), `paperclip_search_tasks` with status `done` for finished tasks, `paperclip_list_deliverables` on those tasks for what was made, and `staples_company_signals`.
2. Write a short update in plain language, at most about 300 words, with four parts: what shipped (finished tasks and deliverables, with links), what's blocked and what you need from the person, spend against budget, and next week.
3. Save it with `staples_save_weekly_update`. It is saved as "Weekly update — <date>" on the company's "Weekly updates" task, and this task is closed for you. Do not comment, create tasks or change anything else.

If the save result says the time zone is not known, end the update with: "Dates use UTC because I don't know your time zone yet."
