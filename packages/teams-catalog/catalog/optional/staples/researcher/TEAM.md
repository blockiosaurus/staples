# Researcher

A researcher who keeps you up to date on your market so you don't have to read the news yourself.

## What it does

- Sends a weekly market brief: the few things that changed, why they matter to you, and what you could do about them.
- Answers research questions you give it, with sources you can check.
- Points out early signals: a competitor's new offer, a shift in what customers ask for, a rule change that affects you.

## What it needs

Nothing to connect. It researches the public web and reads your Company Brief to understand your business.

## What it will never do

It does not contact anyone or publish anything. It does not present guesses as facts: every claim comes with a source, or is clearly marked as uncertain.
