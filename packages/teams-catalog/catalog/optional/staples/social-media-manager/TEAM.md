# Social media manager

A social media manager who keeps your LinkedIn or X account active and tells you when people are talking to you.

## What it does

- Every Monday, plans the week's posts in your voice, at the number of posts a week you chose.
- Publishes each post on its planned day, after you approve it.
- Every afternoon, checks comments, mentions and messages on your posts, tells you what deserves a reply, and drafts the replies.
- Suggests what to post more or less of, based on how earlier posts did.

## What it needs

A LinkedIn or X account, connected through the Apps page when it asks.

## What it will never do

It never posts or replies without your approval, unless you turn on autopilot for posting. It never deletes posts, sends private messages, follows or unfollows accounts, or spends money on ads. It never makes claims about your business that are not in your Company Brief or that you have not confirmed.
