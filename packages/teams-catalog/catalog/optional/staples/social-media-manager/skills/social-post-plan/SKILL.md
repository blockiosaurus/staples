# Social Post Plan

A good week of posts is varied, true, and sounds like the business. The bar: the owner can approve the whole plan in five minutes without rewriting anything.

## When to use

- The weekly post plan routine.
- The owner asks for "some posts about X".
- The first two-week plan after the profile audit.

## Procedure

1. **Look back.** Read last week's plan and the engagement notes. Which posts got replies, shares or clicks? What did people ask?
2. **Pick the mix.** For the week's number of posts, aim for roughly:
   - one third useful (a tip, a how-to, an answer to a common question);
   - one third about the business (a product, a customer story the owner confirmed, behind the scenes);
   - one third conversation (a question, an opinion, a reaction to something timely in the industry).
3. **Spread them out.** One post a day per platform where possible. If the platform can schedule posts, pick a day and time (weekday mornings for LinkedIn, varied times for X, unless past posts show better). Otherwise plan weekdays only: queued posts go out at the afternoon engagement check, so they have a day but no time. With more posts than weekdays, put two on some days, on different platforms when you can.
4. **Draft each post** (rules below).
5. **Give each post a status:** "queued" (ready, planned day set), "needs your input" (a fact to confirm), or "draft" (no platform connected).
6. **Write the plan** in the format below.

## Writing a post

- **LinkedIn:** 600 to 1,300 characters. A strong first line, short paragraphs, one idea, a clear ending (a question or a next step). At most 3 hashtags, at the end.
- **X:** under 280 characters. One idea. At most 1 hashtag. A thread only if the owner asked for one.
- **Friendly voice:** warm, plain words, "we" and "you". **Expert:** useful specifics, no hype. **Bold:** short sentences, a clear opinion, never rude.
- Use the Company Brief's words. Never invent numbers, quotes, reviews or results. If a post needs a fact you do not have, write `[confirm: ...]` and mark it "needs your input".
- Suggest an image in words ("photo of the team packing orders") instead of using one you do not have rights to.
- Links: only the business's own pages, or a page you read and checked.

## Plan format

```md
# Post plan — week of <date>

**This week:** <one sentence: the theme and the number of posts>

| Day | Time | Platform | Topic | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | 10:30 | LinkedIn | <topic> | scheduled |
| Tue | afternoon | X | <topic> | queued |

Scheduled posts carry the time set on the platform. Queued posts have no time: they publish at the afternoon engagement check on their day.

## <Day> — <Platform> — <Topic>
<Full post text>
Image idea: <optional>
Facts to confirm: <none, or the list>

## Last week
<Two or three lines: what did well, what to change.>
```

## Rules

- Never publish from the plan task itself unless the platform schedules the post for its planned day.
- Never repeat a post from the last four weeks.
- No politics, tragedies or sensitive news unless the owner asked.
