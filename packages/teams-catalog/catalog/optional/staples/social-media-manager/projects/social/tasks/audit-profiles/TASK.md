Get to know {{company.name}}'s social accounts and propose the first two weeks of posts.

If no platform is connected yet, wait: work your "Connect <service>" task first, then come back to this one.

1. Read the Company Brief and the connected profiles (chosen platforms: {{answers.platforms}}): the bio, the last 20 posts, and how each did.
2. Write a task document `report` with:
   - "Your profiles today": what each profile says, how often it posts, and what got the most response.
   - "Quick fixes": up to 5 suggestions for the bio, links or pinned post. Do not change the profile; editing it is an outward action and the person decides.
   - "What to post about": three to five themes that fit the Company Brief and the chosen voice ({{answers.voice}}).
   - "The next 2 weeks": the chosen number of posts ({{answers.postsPerWeek}}), each with a day, a platform and the full draft text.
3. Ask {{person.name}} in one question card:
   - Whether the themes are right (multiple choice, the ones you recommend marked).
   - Whether to queue the 2-week plan as written (recommended: "Yes, queue it").
4. Comment with a short summary and a link to the document, then set this task to `in_review` while you wait.

When they answer, copy the approved posts into a `post-plan` document with planned days, marked "queued", and mark this task done. Nothing is published from this task; posts go out on their planned days, each after approval.
