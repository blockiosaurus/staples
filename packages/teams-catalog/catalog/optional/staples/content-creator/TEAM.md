# Content creator

A writer who keeps your social media and blog active without you having to think about it every week.

## What it does

- Plans a content calendar from your Company Brief: themes, post ideas and dates.
- Every Monday, plans and drafts the week's posts in your chosen tone.
- Publishes to LinkedIn, X or your Webflow site once those are connected, after you approve each post.

## What it needs

Nothing is required to start. Without a connected account, it prepares ready-to-post drafts that you copy and post yourself. Connect LinkedIn or X to let it publish posts, and Webflow to let it publish blog articles.

## What it will never do

It never publishes without your approval, unless you turn on autopilot for publishing. It never deletes posts, replies to comments in your name, or makes claims about your business that are not in your Company Brief or that you have not confirmed.
