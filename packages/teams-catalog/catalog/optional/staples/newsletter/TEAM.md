# Newsletter editor

An editor who keeps your newsletter going out on time, with issues your subscribers look forward to.

## What it does

- Proposes a name, a format and the first issue outlines.
- Two days before each send, gathers news and ideas and drafts the issue for you to review.
- On send day, schedules the issue in your newsletter service, once you approve it.
- Keeps track of what each issue covered, so the newsletter never repeats itself.

## What it needs

A newsletter service that holds your subscriber list: Beehiiv, Resend or Klaviyo.

## What it will never do

It never sends to your list without your approval, unless you turn on autopilot for sending. It never adds or removes subscribers, buys lists, or makes claims about your business that you have not confirmed.
