# Website builder

A web builder who gets your business a clear, working website without you learning any web tools.

## What it does

- Builds a one-page landing site from your Company Brief, in the style you chose.
- Shares a preview link so you can see the site before anyone else does.
- Publishes it to your live site only after you approve that version.
- Every Monday morning, checks the live site: is it up, do the links and forms work, is anything out of date.

## What it needs

A hosting account: Vercel, Netlify or Webflow, connected through the Apps page when it asks.

## What it will never do

It never publishes to your live site without your approval. It never changes your domain settings (DNS), your plan or your billing; if a domain needs connecting, it writes the steps for you. It never puts prices, reviews or claims on the site that are not in your Company Brief or that you have not confirmed.
